# Design direction: Field / Forward

A personal portfolio for Samir Chaulagain, a senior civil engineer and community leader in Hamilton, New Zealand. The primary audience is clients, collaborators, employers and community partners. The primary journey is understand Samir, explore his practice, then connect.

## Visual system

- Chalk #F5F7F8: main canvas, clear rather than nostalgic.
- Drawing ink #193340: headings and high-contrast text, drawn from technical drawing.
- Survey blue #245A72: engineering illustrations and the digital lab.
- Sky #DDEAF0: generous photographic and illustration backdrops.
- Site orange #F26738: small wayfinding accents and geometric details; never small text on white.
- Slate #536672: body copy with sufficient contrast.

Barlow Condensed is the large display face: tall, confident lettering with a relationship to drawing-sheet titles. Manrope is the reading and interface face. Both fonts are served locally.

## Composition

One memorable opening: oversized type next to Samir’s real graduation portrait. An asymmetric portfolio follows, using an original civil site-plan illustration, a locally rendered tool preview and a community identity composition. Each has its own scale and purpose. Body copy stays short and left aligned. Longer background and practice detail live on separate pages.

An early cream/serif direction was rejected because it was interchangeable with creative-studio templates. The final palette and condensed typography connect more directly to engineering drawings and the New Zealand setting. Repeated counters, theme pickers, glowing cards, decorative maps, automatic carousels and fake testimonials are omitted.

## UX

- Static HTML content, real links and full navigation work without JavaScript.
- A compact menu on small screens, keyboard focus, native accessible controls.
- Clear separation of civil practice, community leadership and digital work.
- Four working lab tools use local calculations; state is reflected in the URL.
- Motion is short, restrained and disabled for reduced-motion preferences.
- No scroll hijacking, cursor replacement or compulsory loading screen.
- No fictional client projects. Visuals are explicitly original practice illustrations.
- Contact uses a verified public profile; no dead email or pretend submission form.
